Planets in Astrology

Sun: Core identity, ego, life purpose, vitality, and conscious will. It represents the self and the "hero's journey."
Moon: Emotional needs, subconscious habits, instincts, maternal figures, and the private inner life.
Mercury: Communication style, thought processes, logic, learning, siblings, and short trips.
Venus: Love language, values, aesthetics, romance, pleasure, and financial resources.
Mars: Drive, ambition, aggression, physical energy, sexuality, and how one asserts oneself.
Jupiter: Expansion, growth, optimism, philosophy, luck, higher education, and long-distance travel.
Saturn: Discipline, responsibility, limitations, boundaries, structure, karma, and time. The "taskmaster."
Uranus: Innovation, rebellion, sudden changes, originality, technology, and collective breakthroughs.
Neptune: Dreams, illusions, spirituality, intuition, art, deception, and boundary-dissolving experiences.
Pluto: Transformation, power dynamics, the subconscious, death and rebirth, intensity, and deep psychological healing.
North Node (Rahu): Life purpose, destiny, karmic lessons to learn in this lifetime, what we are moving towards.
South Node (Ketu): Past life karma, comfort zones, innate talents, what we need to move away from or integrate.
